of(BuildContext context) → LumenThemeExt
Gets the active neon palette and style for context. Search order: nearest LumenScope, then registered extension in ThemeData, and finally the default palette (LumenPalette.defaultDark or LumenPalette.light, depending on the brightness of the app theme) with LumenStyle.neon. This way widgets work in apps that keep the own ThemeData and never record anything from the kit.
