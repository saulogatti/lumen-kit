Creates a complete palette for the neon theme. Gradients are optional: when omitted, they are derived from the background and accents, and monitor any changes made via copyWith. accentCyan, accentMagenta and accentBlue are old aliases of primary, secondary and tertiary, maintained for compatibility only.
